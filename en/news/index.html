Puerto Santo Domingo news

Updates from the terminal and materials for journalists and counterparties.

SampleJanuary 26, 2027

24/7 operations and automated loading in Villeta

PSD dispatches up to 8,000 m³ per day across 8 islands and 16 bays, with fully automated loading and radar inventory gauging in every tank.

SampleJanuary 12, 2027

PSD plans to expand storage to 158,800 m³

The Villeta terminal plans to add 71,800 m³ to its installed capacity of 87,000 m³. The New Business team is open to interested investors and lenders.

SampleDecember 15, 2026

Puerto Santo Domingo launches its corporate website

The new website, in English and Spanish, brings together the terminal's technical data, its operations and the projected storage expansion to 158,800 m³.
