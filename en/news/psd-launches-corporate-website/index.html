Puerto Santo Domingo launches its corporate website

The new website, in English and Spanish, brings together the terminal's technical data, its operations and the projected storage expansion to 158,800 m³.

SampleDecember 15, 2026

Villeta, December 15, 2026. Puerto Santo Domingo (PSD), Grupo Martín Martín's fuel port terminal, has launched its corporate website in English and Spanish.

The website is designed for investors, traders, banks, development finance institutions and logistics operators. It brings together the information a counterparty needs to understand the terminal:

  • Infrastructure. The full technical data sheet: 100,000 m² in the Villeta industrial park, 300 m of frontage on the Paraguay River, a berth for two 67 m barges or vessels up to 120 m, and a 13 ft draft.
  • Operations. The sequence of reception, storage, dispatch, bunkering and export, with 24/7 operations and fully automated loading.
  • Safety. The fire protection, inventory control and traceability systems.
  • Investment opportunity. The projected storage expansion from 87,000 m³ to 158,800 m³, and a direct line to the New Business team.

The terminal's figures are shown with their source and the date they were last updated.

Commercial inquiries can be sent through the contact form, and investor inquiries through Investment opportunity.
