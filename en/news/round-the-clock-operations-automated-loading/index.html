24/7 operations and automated loading in Villeta

PSD dispatches up to 8,000 m³ per day across 8 islands and 16 bays, with fully automated loading and radar inventory gauging in every tank.

SampleJanuary 26, 2027

Villeta, January 26, 2027. Puerto Santo Domingo (PSD) operates around the clock, every day of the year, with fully automated truck loading.

The terminal's loading rack has 8 islands and 16 bays, with a dispatch capacity of 8,000 m³ per day. Automation reduces manual tasks in every load and, together with radar gauging and automatic reconciliation in each tank, keeps inventory under control throughout the operation.

Five operations on one site.

  • Reception of diesel and gasoline through two discharge lines running from the berth to the tanks.
  • Storage in 87,000 m³ of installed capacity.
  • Dispatch by truck, at 8,000 m³ per day.
  • Bunkering to vessels, at a pumping rate of 80 m³/h.
  • Export of ethanol and biodiesel through two lines, at a pumping rate of 350 m³/h.

Each operation is described in detail under Operations.
