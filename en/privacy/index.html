Privacy policy

How Fuelpar S.A. processes the personal data of website visitors and of people who write to us.

Effective from December 15, 2026

Draft for Legal review.

This policy explains how Fuelpar S.A. processes the personal data of visitors to the Puerto Santo Domingo (PSD) website and of people who write to it through the website's forms. It applies to both the English and Spanish versions of the website.

1. Data controller

  • Controller: Fuelpar S.A.
  • Taxpayer ID (RUC): [RUC number]
  • Registered address: [registered address]
  • Privacy contact: privacidad@psdparaguay.com

2. Legal framework

Fuelpar S.A. processes personal data in accordance with Law No. 7593/2025 on Personal Data Protection of the Republic of Paraguay. Where processing involves people located in the European Union, in particular through website analytics, Regulation (EU) 2016/679, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), also applies.

The website was designed to meet the stricter of the two standards: no data is used for analytics without the visitor's prior consent.

Representative in the European Union (Article 27 GDPR): not appointed, as the processing is occasional.

3. Data we process

  • Data you provide in the forms. Contact form: full name, company, country, email address and message. Investor form: full name, organization, position, country, email address, phone number (optional), type of interest and message.
  • Technical browsing data. The IP address and browser data received by the hosting service to deliver pages and protect the website.
  • Anti-spam data. On pages with a form, Cloudflare Turnstile receives your IP address and technical browser signals to tell a person from an automated program.
  • Analytics data, only if you accept it. Google Analytics 4 records how the website is used, such as pages viewed, language, device type, approximate location and successful form submissions, without their content. Analytics never includes your name, email address or the text of your messages.
  • Record of your cookie choice. A random identifier, the date and time, your choice for each category, the language and the version of the policy in force. It does not include your IP address.

The forms do not accept attachments. Please do not include sensitive data, such as health information, beliefs or political opinions, in your messages.

Fuelpar S.A. does not use your data for advertising, does not build profiles and does not make automated decisions that affect you. The forms do not subscribe you to any mailing list.

6. International transfers

Microsoft, Cloudflare and Google may process data outside Paraguay and the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Fuelpar S.A. relies on the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, and the safeguards required by Law No. 7593/2025 for these transfers. You can request information on these safeguards at privacidad@psdparaguay.com.

8. Your rights

Under Law No. 7593/2025 and the GDPR, you may:

  • access your data and find out how it is processed;
  • ask for it to be corrected or updated;
  • ask for it to be erased;
  • object to processing or ask for it to be restricted;
  • receive it in a structured, commonly used format (portability);
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting prior processing. For analytics, simply change your choice under “Cookie preferences” at the bottom of any page.

To exercise these rights, write to privacidad@psdparaguay.com stating which right you wish to exercise and providing details that allow us to identify you. We may ask you to verify your identity. There is no charge, and we will reply within one month of receiving your request.

If you believe your data is not being processed lawfully, you may lodge a complaint with [supervisory authority designated under Law No. 7593/2025] and, if you are in the European Union, with the data protection authority of your Member State.

9. Security

The website is served over HTTPS only. The forms are protected against automated submissions and deliver messages directly to Microsoft 365 mailboxes, without storing them along the way. Mailbox access is limited to the people who answer inquiries, and technical logs do not contain the text of messages.

10. Minors

The website is intended for businesses and professionals. It is not directed at people under 18, and Fuelpar S.A. does not knowingly process data relating to minors.

11. Changes to this policy

Fuelpar S.A. publishes any changes on this page, with the new effective date. If a change affects analytics or cookies, the website will ask for your choice again.

12. Language

This policy is published in Spanish and English. In the event of any discrepancy between the two versions, the Spanish version prevails.
