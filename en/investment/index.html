Investment opportunity

An operating terminal with room to grow

Puerto Santo Domingo plans to expand its storage from 87,000 m³ to 158,800 m³. Our New Business team works with funds, banks, traders and development finance institutions interested in the project.

Capacity

From 87,000 m³ to 158,800 m³ of storage

The terminal plans to add 71,800 m³ of storage, 83% above its installed capacity. The expansion builds on a running operation: a berth on the Paraguay River, 8,000 m³ per day of dispatch capacity, fully automated loading and 24/7 operations.

Why PSD

What counterparties assess at the terminal

  • An operating asset

    PSD already receives, stores and dispatches fuels around the clock. The expansion builds on infrastructure and processes in place today, not on a greenfield project.

  • River and road access

    300 m of frontage on the Paraguay River, a berth for two 67 m barges or vessels up to 120 m, and truck dispatch across 8 islands and 16 bays.

  • Dispatch at scale

    A capacity of 8,000 m³ per day, with fully automated loading.

  • Several service lines

    Reception of diesel and gasoline, storage, truck dispatch, bunkering at 80 m³/h and ethanol and biodiesel exports at 350 m³/h.

  • Measurable inventory control

    Radar gauging and automatic reconciliation in every tank: a verifiable basis for anyone financing or trading stored product.

  • A direct point of contact

    PSD is a proprietary asset of Grupo Martín Martín. Our New Business team is the dedicated channel for investors and lenders.
