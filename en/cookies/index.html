Cookie policy

Which cookies the website uses, what they are for and how to decide about them.

Effective from December 15, 2026

Draft for Legal review.

This policy explains which cookies the Puerto Santo Domingo website uses, what they are for, how long they last and how you can accept them, reject them or change your choice. The controller is Fuelpar S.A. Data obtained through cookies is processed in accordance with the Privacy policy.

1. What a cookie is

A cookie is a small file that a website stores in your browser to remember information from one page to the next, or from one visit to the next. Some cookies are necessary for the website to work; others, such as analytics cookies, are only used if you allow them.

2. How this website uses cookies

  • No advertising cookies. The website does not use advertising or social media cookies and does not share data for advertising purposes.
  • Analytics only with your permission. Google Analytics 4 does not load until you select “Accept”. If you reject or close the banner, it does not load.
  • No third parties in the background. The typeface is hosted on the website itself, the hosting service sets no tracking cookies, and no third-party maps or videos load without action on your part.
  • Anti-spam only where needed. Cloudflare Turnstile loads only on pages with a form.

4. Google Analytics 4 on this website

When you accept analytics, the website uses Google Analytics 4 with the following settings:

  • Google consent mode in its basic implementation, so the Google tag does not load before you accept;
  • advertising signals always denied, because the website does not advertise;
  • Google Signals and ad personalization turned off;
  • no user IDs and no personal data in events or page addresses;
  • data retention of 14 months.

5. How to accept, reject or change your choice

On your first visit, the website shows a banner with three options of equal prominence: “Accept”, “Reject” and “Settings”. Closing the banner counts as rejecting, and the banner does not prevent you from reading the website.

Your choice is stored for 12 months. You can change it at any time through the “Cookie preferences” link at the bottom of every page. If you withdraw your consent, the website stops loading Google Analytics 4 and deletes the _ga cookies from your browser.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. If you block necessary cookies, the website will not be able to remember your choice and will show you the banner again.

6. Changes to this policy

If the list of cookies changes, Fuelpar S.A. will update this policy with a new effective date, and the website will ask for your choice again even if 12 months have not passed.

7. Contact

For any questions about cookies or personal data, write to privacidad@psdparaguay.com.

8. Language

This policy is published in Spanish and English. In the event of any discrepancy between the two versions, the Spanish version prevails.
