Sustainability

Environment, safety and community

How Puerto Santo Domingo manages the effects of its operations on the river, on people and on the city of Villeta.

Approach

Three areas, one operation

The sustainability of a fuel terminal is decided in its day-to-day operations: in how product is received, stored and dispatched, in how incidents are prevented and in how the terminal coexists with the city around it.

PSD organizes its management in three areas:

  • Environment: the terminal's environmental license and product control at every stage.
  • Safety: protection of people, facilities and product.
  • Villeta community: the relationship with the city where the terminal operates.

PSD will publish indicators for each area once a year, with their source and date.

Environment

Environmental license and product control

Environmental license. The terminal's environmental license is Environmental Impact Declaration DIA 3495/2021 (MADES).

Product control. Radar gauging and automatic reconciliation in every tank make it possible to detect any inventory discrepancy early. At a fuel terminal, that control is also the first barrier against product loss to the environment.

Environmental management. [environmental monitoring programs, waste and effluent management, and spill contingency plan]

Biofuels. Through its two export lines, the terminal ships ethanol and biodiesel.

Safety

People, facilities and product

Fire prevention. Foam fire-suppression systems.

Product control. Radar inventory gauging, automatic reconciliation and traceability from reception to dispatch.

Physical security. Site access control and CCTV surveillance.

Occupational safety. [occupational safety indicators for the terminal]

These systems are described in detail under Safety.

Villeta community

A terminal that is part of the city

Puerto Santo Domingo is located in the Villeta industrial park, in the Central Department, 33 km from Asunción. The terminal shares the river and the access roads with the city, so the safety of its operations is also part of its relationship with its neighbors.

[local employment, local suppliers and programs with the Villeta community]

Residents and institutions in Villeta can reach the terminal through the contact form.
