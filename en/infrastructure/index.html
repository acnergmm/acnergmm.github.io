Terminal technical data sheet

Site, berth, storage, dispatch, pumping and control: Puerto Santo Domingo's key data, with source and date.

An integrated asset

Berth, tank farm and loading rack on one site

PSD's infrastructure brings three components together on 100,000 m² of the Villeta industrial park: the berth on the Paraguay River, the tank farm and the truck loading rack. Product lines connect the berth with the tanks, for both discharge and export.

The data sheet below summarizes the capacity of each component. The figures come from PSD's corporate brochure and are current as of October 2026.

Scope of the information

What the data sheet covers, and what it does not

The data sheet describes capacities, dimensions and systems. It does not disclose equipment locations or the configuration of protection and surveillance systems, as that information is part of the terminal's security.

The 158,800 m³ maximum capacity is a PSD estimate. The scope of the expansion is set out under Investment opportunity.
